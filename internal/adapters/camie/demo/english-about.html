The China Association of Machinery Industry for Environmental Protection (CAMIE) was founded in 1994. It consists of manufacturers of environmental protection equipment and resource utilization equipment, as well as enterprises and institutions engaged in technology development, design, and technical services in the field of environmental pollution control. Upholding its mission of serving the government, industry, and enterprises, the association has long been engaged in policy research, achievement promotion, standard formulation, and international cooperation for China's environmental protection equipment manufacturing sector. It also provides insights and recommendations for major government decisions and member organizations.

After more than 30 years of development, CAMIE has become one of the most influential national industry associations in China's environmental protection equipment manufacturing industry. The association is committed to promoting the healthy development of low carbon environmental protection and resource utilization equipment manufacturing by strengthening coordination among the government, industry, and enterprises. It aims to accelerate the sector's high quality development through standards and technological innovation, support its green, digital, intelligent, and international transformation, and advance sustainable, low carbon growth.
